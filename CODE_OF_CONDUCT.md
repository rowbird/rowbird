# Code of conduct

Rowbird follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, accept constructive criticism
gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political
attacks, sexualized language and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in the
repository, issues, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **conduct@rowbird.dev**. Reports are handled confidentially by the
maintainers, who will review them promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
contributions and may warn, suspend or ban participants, following the enforcement guidelines of
the Contributor Covenant.
